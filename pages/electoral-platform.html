Project: Tibetan Exile Electoral Platform

In collaboration with Tibet Times (བོད་ཀྱི་དུས་བབ།), TYI contributed electoral analytic research and digital optimization guidance for the widely used public results platform covering the 2025–2026 Tibetan exile general elections, including Sikyong and Parliament-in-Exile races.

The platform became a primary public reference for election results during the cycle, giving Tibetan communities worldwide a single bilingual interface for Sikyong totals, regional vote shares, parliamentary outcomes, and maps of polling geography.

TYI’s Role

TYI supported the project through electoral analytic research and recommendations on digital infrastructure for diaspora democracy. The research analyzed structural barriers to participation under Central Tibetan Administration elections and proposed a phased strategy for secure, accessible digital tools.

Research Brief

Tibetan Presidential Elections: Analysis and Digital Optimization
By Nyida Gyal

The brief examines the Sikyong election system as a model of stateless democracy spanning nearly 30 countries. It identifies structural barriers (candidate access, diaspora inequality, documentation gaps), participation challenges (geographic dispersion, youth disengagement, language access), and technological deficits (limited interactive tools, cybersecurity, digital divide). It then outlines short-, medium-, and long-term optimization proposals, including voter education, centralized candidate information, digitized registration, and staged online voting with security and accessibility requirements.

What the Platform Delivers

  • Sikyong results with candidate totals, percentages, and regional vote share (India/Nepal/Bhutan, Americas, Europe & Africa, Australasia)
  • State-level India breakdowns for major Tibetan settlement regions
  • Parliament (Chitue) results by religious schools and traditional provinces (U-Tsang, Dotoe, Domey) and diaspora constituencies
  • Interactive election map of polling geography worldwide
  • Bilingual Tibetan–English interface for community-wide accessibility

Platform publisher: Tibet Times / Bod-Kyi-Dus-Bab, Dharamsala. Live site: elections.tibettimes.net

Why This Matters

Exile democracy only works if voters can find clear, trusted information. By pairing analytic research with a public-facing results system, TYI helped strengthen civic access for Tibetan communities far beyond any single school or campus program, connecting youth-led research capacity to a diaspora institution with global reach.
